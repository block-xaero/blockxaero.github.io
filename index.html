A proposed deploymenton your premisesor in the cloudin phases

How Cyan would be deployed: the work travels to your footage, the record travels everywhere.

Two ways to run Cyan, and we favor the first. On your premises, the way we have built it twice: a rack beside your storage, a heavy room, a regional room. Or in the cloud, the way Cyan runs today: you edit, color or observe from anywhere, and the floor keeps the beat. Either way raw media stays where you keep it, the seats share one record, and only small things travel between rooms.

Your footage stays put

Engines run where the media root lives, your floor or your cloud storage. Conform, grade and render happen beside it.

The record is yours

Boards, notes, approvals and the cut's history sync between your seats, on your rack or in your cloud account, never on ours.

Only small things cross

Proxies, looks, comments, approvals and workflow definitions move between rooms. Masters are delivered from the site that made them.

A second studio is the same playbook beside its own media. A rack grows when sustained GPU use passes seventy percent; a cloud room grows by the hour. Either way, every step carries its time and its cost.

  • A rack slot with power, or a cloud account, for the first room.
  • The seats' machines and their DCC licenses: Premiere, Resolve, Pro Tools.
