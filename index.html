Notebook = Workflow

Each cell is a step. Write what needs to happen in plain English. Lens compiles your intent into an executable pipeline — no YAML, no drag-and-drop, no code.

Board = Live Status

The board face shows every step as a live dot. Click any dot to see what the AI did, what's pending, and who needs to act. Your team sees progress in real time.

Humans in the Loop

When a step needs human eyes, Lens messages the right people through Cyan chat. They review, approve, and the pipeline continues. No context switching.
