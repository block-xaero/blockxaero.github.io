A proposed deploymenton your floorin phases

How Cyan would be deployed: the work travels to your footage, the record travels everywhere.

This is how Cyan would potentially be deployed for a floor like yours. It runs beside your storage: raw media never leaves your site, the seats share one record, and only small things travel between racks. It starts as half a rack and grows by adding racks.

Your footage stays put

Engines run at the site that holds the media root. Conform, grade and render happen against your storage, on your floor.

The record is yours, and cloudless

Boards, notes, approvals and the cut's history sync between your seats. A seat needs a link to the other seats, not to a cloud.

Only small things cross

Proxies, looks, comments, approvals and workflow definitions move between rooms. Masters are delivered from the site that made them.

A phased build-out, as proposed

PhaseWhereWhat would runScale, as an estimate
OneYour site, half a rackThe engine and its database on one machine, one reading model, working storageOne or two engines, single-digit seats, about five kilowatts
TwoThe heavy room, a full rackGPU render workers, larger judging models, the activity log and usage metering, the hot cacheAbout sixteen GPUs within a ten-kilowatt rack, repeatable per rack
ThreeA regional siteRegional seats with their own permissions, overflow render, an optional shared model gatewaySeats and burst render; the main floor is never dependent on it

A second studio with its own storage is the same playbook: a room beside their media, on their own record. New hardware is needed only when sustained GPU use passes seventy percent.
