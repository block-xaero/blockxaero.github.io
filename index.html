Division of labor

AI does the work.
Humans make the calls.

AI handles

  • Scans hours of video in seconds
  • Regional compliance checks
  • Multi-language subtitle generation
  • Ad break optimization
  • Quality control across format and audio
  • Responds to questions about findings in context

Humans decide

  • Is this profanity intentional? Director's choice.
  • Move the ad break — the scene is too emotional.
  • The bleep sounds harsh — use a music sting instead.
  • Approve for one region, hold for another's review.
  • Ask AI: "What if we cut this scene?"
  • Final sign-off before delivery.
